Meet Dr. Smiles

Dr. Smiles Uganda is the dental practice of Dr. John Vianney Serumaga, a dentist focused on diagnosing oral disease early and helping people keep their teeth healthy for life.

The approach is simple: catch problems before they grow, explain every step in plain language, and keep good dental care within reach. That's why prevention and patient education sit at the heart of every visit, and why Dr. Smiles regularly takes dentistry out of the clinic and into communities through mobile services and charity projects.

Smile school

Short guides from the Dr. Smiles feed, plus everyday habits that protect your teeth.

Need a tooth extraction? What to know before a tooth is removed. Why regular scaling matters Tartar can't be brushed away. Scaling removes it. What stains your teeth? Smoking, coffee, wine, chocolate and sweets.

Four everyday habits

  • Brush twice a day for about two minutes with a fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean between your teeth once a day with floss or interdental brushes.
  • Go easy on sugary snacks and drinks, especially between meals.
  • Book regular check-ups so small problems are found while they're easy to fix.

General guidance only. For advice about your own teeth, book an examination.
